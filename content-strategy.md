# 8b-is Content Strategy: Expanded Channel Plan & KPIs

**Document Purpose:** Operationalize the Graylab Content Pillar Framework into a measurable channel plan  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Created:** 2026-10-27  

---

## Executive Summary

This document translates the five Graylab content pillars into specific channel strategies, content production workflows, and KPIs for the launch phase. The goal is to establish 8b-is as a credible, recognizable voice at the intersection of institutional finance and modern technology.

---

## Phase 1: Launch (Months 1-3)

### Objectives
1. Establish baseline brand awareness across all 5 pillars
2. Build an engaged initial community of ~500 followers across platforms
3. Produce a content backlog of 60+ publishable assets
4. Identify top 2 performing content pillars for focus in Phase 2

### Channel Deep-Dive

#### Twitter/X (Primary Revenue & Distribution Channel)

**Role:** Real-time market commentary, networking with institutional players, lead generation to newsletter and GitHub

**Posting Cadence:** 1-2 tweets + 1 thread per week
**Content Mix:**
- 40% Market Intelligence (data-driven insights, chart breakdowns)
- 25% Digital Transition (building in public narratives)
- 20% Institutional Wisdom (lessons learned, mental models)
- 15% Community/Tool announcements (GitHub releases, Discord updates)

**KPIs:**
- Follower growth: target +300 organic followers (Month 1), doubling by Month 3
- Engagement rate: >3% on threads, >1.5% on individual tweets
- Profile visits to Linktree conversion rate: >8%

#### LinkedIn (B2B Credibility & Institutional Audience)

**Role:** Building institutional credibility, attracting high-profile network, partnership opportunities

**Posting Cadence:** 3 posts/week + daily engagement in relevant groups/comments
**Content Mix:**
- 50% Institutional Wisdom (lessons from Wall Street career)
- 30% Digital Transition (tradition to tech bridge narratives)
- 20% Market Intelligence summaries (translated for professional audience)

**KPIs:**
- Connection growth: +100 relevant contacts/month
- Post reach: first post >500 impressions, target 2K+ by Month 3
- Profile views from non-network connections: track trend upward

#### Substack Newsletter (Content Anchor & Owned Audience)

**Role:** Deep-dive home for Market Intelligence + Institutional Wisdom, capture owned email list

**Cadence:** Weekly (Thursday AM ET)
**Title Draft:** *"The Graylab Brief"*

**Issue Structure Template:**
```
Issue #[number] - [Week/Date]

[2-3 sentence hook summarizing the week]

🔍 This Week's Signals:
[Brief 3 key market insights, each with data point]

💡 Core Insight of the Week:
[Deep-dive article, 800-1200 words]

🛠 From the Build Log:
[Tool/update from engineering work]

Next week on [Newsletter title]: what we're watching...
```

**KPIs:**
- Subscriber growth: Month 1 target 50 subscribers doubling each month to reach ~400 by end of Phase 3
- Open rate target: >40% (industry average is ~20%)
- Click-through rate on GitHub/blog links: >8%

#### GitHub / Developer Ecosystem (Technical Authority)

**Role:** Demonstrate engineering depth, attract developer talent, build open-source credibility

**Strategy:**
1. Release 1 micro-tool library per month in Phase 1
2. Document architecture decisions thoroughly (README.md as marketing asset)
3. Engage with dev communities on Reddit (r/algotrading, r/learnpython), Hacker News when releasing technical content

**KPIs:**
- Repository stars: first public repo target 100+, 500+ by Phase 2 close
- Contributors: 2-3 active external contributors by Phase 2
- Issue/discussion engagement rate: >15% of issues receive substantive community response

#### Discord Community (Retention & Deep Engagement)

**Role:** Create sticky community for most engaged followers, facilitate peer discussions

**Launch Timeline:** Begin Month 2 after initial audience is seeded on Twitter/X and LinkedIn

**Initial Channels to Open:**
- `#announcements` (automated or admin only)
- `#market-chat` (casual market discussion)
- `#research-share` (community sharing analysis)
- `#dev-help` (technical support for open-source tools)
- `#off-topic` (general finance and life discussion)

**KPIs:**
- Members: 150 by end of Phase 1, ~500 by Phase 2 close
- Active daily users (DAU/MAU): >20% engagement ratio is strong target
- First community-generated content event by Month 3 (e.g., weekly research show-and-tell)

## Content Production Workflow

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Monday | Weekly planning call / async update | PM/Creator |
| Tuesday | Research & data gathering for Market Intelligence brief | Analyst |
| Wednesday | Draft newsletter article #1 (Core Insight) | Writer/PM |
| Thursday | Newsletter publish + social promotion plan execute | Newsletter ops |
| Friday | Engineering log write-up + GitHub update commit | Engineering |
| Weekend | Batch create Twitter content for following week + schedule via Buffer/TweetDeck | Social/Mktg |
